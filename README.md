# HR-Analysis-Dashboard
The HR Analytics Dashboard is a Business Intelligence project developed using Power BI to analyze employee data and generate actionable insights related to workforce performance, employee demographics, and attrition.

# Dataset
<a href="https://github.com/Rani-Swamy/HR-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/HR_Analytics-4.xlsx">HR DataSet</a>

#KPI	#Purpose
Total Employees	Displays the total workforce in the organization.
Active Employees	Shows the number of employees currently working.
Employees Left	Indicates the number of employees who have left the organization.
Attrition Rate	Measures the percentage of employees who left the company.
Average Age	Shows the average age of employees.
Average Monthly Income	Displays the average monthly salary of employees.
Average Total Working Years	Shows the average work experience of employees.
Average Job Satisfaction	Measures overall employee satisfaction.
Overtime Percentage	Indicates the percentage of employees working overtime.
Performance Rating	Shows the average employee performance rating.
